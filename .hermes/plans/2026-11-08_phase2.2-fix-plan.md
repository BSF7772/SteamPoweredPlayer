## 1. Findings

| ID | Area | Item (maintainer's words, condensed) | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Player | **A-B loop completely broken:** the loop is much shorter than the marked A-B range. | Playback loops exactly from A to B (± 1 frame) on clips and cache sessions, at 1× and other speeds, after zoom, after a seek-by-reload. |
| P2 | Player | **Frame backward doesn't work:** the timestamp jumps and is not reliable. | Each frame-back moves exactly one frame (FF − 1, wrapping at the second), including across a chunk boundary (3 s) and a seek-by-reload; the shown frame and the timecode agree. |
| P3 | Player | At the **default window size** the fullscreen button is clipped. **All controls must show at all times.** | Every transport control (play, −5, frame ±, +5, timecode, A-B, speed, volume, zoom, fullscreen) is fully visible at the default window size and at the window's minimum size; narrow widths shrink/collapse (e.g. volume slider into its button's flyout) instead of clipping. Set a sensible window `MinWidth`/`MinHeight` if needed. |
| P4 | Timeline | Add a **ruler** like Steam's timeline (major ticks + ~10 minor ticks per major interval), and it must **handle zoom well**. | Major tick spacing chosen from a "nice" step ladder (e.g. 1 s, 2 s, 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1 m, 2 m, 5 m, 10 m, 15 m, 30 m, 1 h) so labels never overlap and stay ~80–160 px apart at every zoom level; time labels on major ticks; the ruler scrolls with the zoomed view; pure tick-layout maths in a testable helper. |
| L1 | Library | **Cache strip time axis looks like it is "from the future":** next morning the strip showed only clock times (e.g. 22:37 – 23:42) without a date. | Combine both methods: **exact start date/time** (Today / Yesterday / weekday + date) **and relative age** ("10 h ago"). The axis ends at **now**. **Cut-off:** content older than 24 h is compressed into the **first (left) 25 %** of the strip with a visible break; the last 24 h use the remaining 75 %. Midnight crossings are marked. |
| L2 | Live follow | While Steam records, the live cache session in the Player has **no thumbnails**: empty filmstrip and a placeholder playlist thumbnail. "See what is the solution." | Live sessions get trickplay **incrementally**: when a new chunk appears (scanner event, no polling), extract its tile from the chunk's first fragment (one IDR decode per 3 s chunk), append it to the sheet and refresh the filmstrip and the playlist/library thumbnail. It must not disturb playback (no dropped frames; report CPU time per tile). Markers/events appear when Steam updates the timeline file (event-driven reload), if Steam writes it during recording; report what Steam actually writes while recording. |

Not re-tested and not blocking: 60 fps gap session (no gap in the maintainer's library), cold first scan (the maintainer won't

## 3. Rules for both workers
- Root cause first, with evidence (numbers, log lines); write the failing test, then the fix. Never weaken or skip a test.
- Prove on the **real library** (read-only, `SPP_REAL_LIBRARY`): `--realcheck` and `--uicheck` get new cases for every item
  (P1: loop length measured from mpv `time-pos` over ≥ 3 loops; P2: 10 consecutive frame-backs incl. across a chunk boundary;
  P3: every transport control's bounds inside the window at default and minimum size; L1: label/axis maths unit tests with a
  fixed "now"; L2: tiles appear for a growing synthetic session; and on a live real session when one is recording, otherwise SKIP).
- Trickplay sheet format: appending tiles to a live sheet must stay readable by the current reader; if the manifest format has to
  change, stop and report (ask-first).
- No personal data in the repo, commits or reports (paths, game names, ids, hardware). Window captures stay outside the repo.
- Budgets: `dotnet test` < 2 min, `--selftest` < 30 s, `--realcheck` < 60 s, `--uicheck` < 90 s.
